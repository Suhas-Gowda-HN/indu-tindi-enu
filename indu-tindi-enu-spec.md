# 🍛 Indu Tindi Enu? – Breakfast Suggestion App

*"What's for breakfast today?"* A mobile/web app that suggests a South Indian (Mandya/Karnataka style) breakfast every day, with a few North Indian and egg dishes.

---

## 1. Core Idea

- Every day has a **fixed pool of 7 dishes** of one category (Rice, Dosa/Idli, or Roti).
- The app picks one **at random** from that day's pool (spin wheel or button).
- Each dish shows **sides** (chutney, gojju, sambar, curd, etc.), calories, protein, time, ingredients and a short recipe.
- A **batter reminder** the evening before a Dosa day, based on what the user wants to eat.
- Non-veg = **egg only**. No chicken or mutton. No mudde or saaru meals.

## 2. Rules

### Categories
| Category | What it includes |
|---|---|
| 🍚 Rice | Baths, pongal, chitranna, puliyogare, upma, avalakki uppittu |
| 🥞 Dosa | Idli, vada and every dosa variety (all batter-based) |
| 🫓 Roti | Akki/ragi rotti, chapati, phulka, puri |

### Rotation rules
1. The same category is never served on two consecutive days.
2. Rice items can repeat after 3 days.
3. Roti days are never within 2 days of each other.
4. Dosa days are spread out, with different types each day.
5. **Bisibele bath: Sundays only.**
6. **Ven pongal:** counts as rice, is frequent, and can also appear on any other day as a bonus.
7. **Egg dishes only on Sun, Tue, Wed and Fri.**
8. Each day has **7 options**.

## 4. 🧂 Batter Reminder Feature

Idli, dosa and vada need batter prepared the **day before**. The app must remind the user the evening before every Dosa day (Sun evening for Mon, Tue evening for Wed, Fri evening for Sat), asking what they feel like eating tomorrow.

### Flow
1. At a set time (default **7:00 PM**, user-adjustable), send a notification:
   *"Tomorrow is Dosa day 🥞. What are you in the mood for?"*
2. Show options based on tomorrow's pool:
   - **Idli type** (idli, thatte idli, button idli, set dosa)
   - **Dosa type** (plain, masala, benne, uttapam, paddu, egg dosa)
   - **Vada** (medu vada, idli-vada combo)
   - **Adai / pesarattu** (dal soaking)
   - **Instant** (rava dosa, rava idli, ragi dosa, maddur vada: no overnight prep)
3. Based on the choice, show a **"Prepare tonight" card** with the batter name, soaking time and fermentation tips.
4. Tomorrow morning, narrow the random pick to dishes that match the batter prepared. If the user says "no batter ready", pick from the instant options only.
5. Tick "batter ready ✅" so the pick never suggests a dish that can't be made.

### Batter prep table
| Batter | Used for | Prep tonight |
|---|---|---|
| Idli batter | Idli, thatte idli, button idli, set dosa | Soak urad dal + idli rice 4-6 hrs, grind, ferment 8-12 hrs |
| Dosa batter | Plain, masala, benne, egg dosa, uttapam, paddu | Soak rice + urad dal + fenugreek 4-6 hrs, grind, ferment 8-12 hrs |
| Urad dal paste | Medu vada, idli-vada combo | Soak urad dal 3-4 hrs, grind fluffy next morning (or tonight, refrigerate) |
| Adai batter | Adai | Soak rice + 3-4 dals 4-6 hrs, grind coarse |
| Moong soak | Pesarattu | Soak whole green moong overnight |
| Rice soak | Neer dosa | Soak raw rice 3-4 hrs, grind fine to thin batter |
| Instant | Rava dosa, rava idli, ragi dosa, maddur vada | No overnight prep (rava soak 15-30 mins) |

> Tip: one big batter batch of idli + dosa lasts 2-3 days in the fridge. Add a "batter in fridge" toggle so the app knows leftover batter exists.

## 5. Features

### MVP
- Today screen with a **spin wheel** over the day's 7 dishes
- Dish card: sides, calories, protein, time, ingredients, short recipe
- Weekly menu screen
- Batter reminder
- Kannada/English toggle
- Weekly grocery list (combined ingredients for the week)
- Reroll button ("Not today") that does not repeat yesterday's dish

### Extras
- **"I'm late" mode:** only dishes of 10 minutes or less
- **Leftover rescue:** type the ingredients you have and get matching dishes
- **Festival mode:** Ugadi (obbattu, ugadi pachadi + idli), Sankranti (sakkare pongal, ven pongal), Ganesha Chaturthi (kadubu, kesari bath)
- **Weather mood:** hot day gives lighter dishes, rainy day gives warm dishes (pongal, baths, rotti)
- **Veg/egg filter:** "veg only today" switch
- **History:** what we ate this week, so it is never boring
- **Share on WhatsApp:** "Today's breakfast: Akki rotti 🫓"

## 7. Pick Algorithm

1. Take today's pool (7 dishes).
2. Remove dishes that need a batter that is not ready.
3. If "I'm late" is on, keep only dishes of 10 minutes or less.
4. Remove yesterday's dish and down-weight anything eaten in the last 3 days.
5. Apply weather weights (optional).
6. Pick randomly by weight and animate the wheel to land on the winner.
