🌿 About "Indu Tindi Enu?"

In Karnataka and South Indian homes, the most asked question every sunrise is: "ಇಂದು ತಿಂಡಿ ಏನು?" (What's for breakfast today?).

This app follows authentic Mandya and Karnataka breakfast culinary traditions: balanced rotation of rice, batter-fermented dosas/idlis, and stone-ground rotis, ensuring nutrition and no breakfast boredom!
